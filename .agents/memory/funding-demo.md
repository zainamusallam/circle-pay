---
name: Funding demo scope
description: Product intent and boundaries for the student presentation's funding simulation.
---

Circle Pay (formerly Jamiyat Digital) is a student-presentation frontend demo. The user requested mock connected funding sources, balances, automatic subscription deductions, and simulated protected balances—not real bank connections or money movement.

**Why:** The requested experience demonstrates rotating savings-circle automation during a live presentation.

**How to apply:** Keep funding simulation local and explicitly labelled. Do not introduce real payment providers or claim actual financial protection without a new user request.

A fully funded month's payout is queued automatically; it remains visibly held until the presenter completes the demo transfer.

**Why:** The request explicitly calls for automatic queuing, and separating queued from released funds makes both stages observable during the presentation.

**How to apply:** Preserve the distinction between contribution receipts, protected balances, queued payouts, and completed recipient transfers.

Use “User” rather than Ahmed in the presentation. The user clarified that 3,000 KWD and 7,000 KWD are circle savings goals, not monthly contribution amounts.

**Why:** The user requested generic presentation identity and explicitly clarified the meaning of the larger figures.

**How to apply:** Calculate the monthly contribution as the circle goal divided by its participant count, not as an unrelated illustrative amount. The user’s example is 7,000 KWD with seven people = 1,000 KWD per person. Keep cumulative contributions separate from this monthly share. Individual joint-account member cards should show names only.

Each circle must have its own separate large joint-account card: membership in two circles means two cards. Use the term “protected balance” instead of “escrow” in the app.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested separate joint accounts per circle and changed the terminology.

**How to apply:** Show only that circle’s deposited funds, members, and payment due dates on its account card. Do not combine personal wallet balances or funds from other circles.

The brand is “Circle Pay.” The first screen should show the name and a rotating logo before entering the app. Do not display a separate “Payout pot” metric.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested the rename, first navigation page, and removal of that duplicate amount.

**How to apply:** Preserve the welcome-entry flow and respect reduced-motion preferences. Keep funding history, protected balances, and contribution figures consistent with goal-based equal shares.

After each project update, remind the user to decide whether to push the changes to GitHub.

**Why:** The user asked to be reminded after project updates.

**How to apply:** Ask whether they want the current changes pushed; do not treat the reminder as automatic permission to push.
