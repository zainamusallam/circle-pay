---
name: Funding demo scope
description: Product intent and boundaries for the student presentation's funding simulation.
---

Jamiyat Digital is a student-presentation frontend demo. The user requested mock connected funding sources, balances, automatic subscription deductions, and simulated escrow—not real bank connections or money movement.

**Why:** The requested experience demonstrates rotating savings-circle automation during a live presentation.

**How to apply:** Keep funding simulation local and explicitly labelled. Do not introduce real payment providers or claim actual escrow security without a new user request.

A fully funded month's payout is queued automatically; it remains visibly held until the presenter completes the demo transfer.

**Why:** The request explicitly calls for automatic queuing, and separating queued from released funds makes both stages observable during the presentation.

**How to apply:** Preserve the distinction between contribution receipts, held escrow, queued payouts, and completed recipient transfers.

Use “User” rather than Ahmed in the presentation. The user clarified that 3,000 KWD and 7,000 KWD are circle savings goals, not monthly contribution amounts.

**Why:** The user requested generic presentation identity and explicitly clarified the meaning of the larger figures.

**How to apply:** Keep goals clearly labelled separately from monthly contributions and payouts. Individual joint-account member cards should show names only; show the combined balance and payment due dates on the large joint-account card.
